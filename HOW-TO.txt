PULSE REDESIGN - STAGE 4 (Trading terminal)

1. Unzip INTO your trading-app folder (the one with package.json). Choose "Replace" when asked.
   It replaces/adds 4 files:
      src/pages/AdminTrading.jsx          (replaced - the new Trading Console / terminal)
      src/components/CandlestickChart.jsx (replaced - chart now fits its space, also fixes Markets)
      src/styles/terminal.css             (new)
      src/styles/shell.css                (updated - readable labels on selected gold buttons)
   Optional safety: copy AdminTrading.jsx and CandlestickChart.jsx somewhere first.
2. Run:  npm run dev   then log in as an admin and open  Trading Console  in the sidebar.
   (Only admins with the "trade" permission can open or close positions - unchanged.)

What you get: instrument tabs, price strip (Live feed / Simulated label), active-session list,
candlestick chart, order panel (Long/Short, margin, quick 25/50/75/100%, confirm step),
tabs for Open positions / Trade history / Session & leverage. Every action works exactly as
before and is still audit-logged by the server.

To undo: put your old AdminTrading.jsx and CandlestickChart.jsx back.
